## 5. Paper-ready scope and next proposal
## 5. 可入文表述及唯一下一提案

In a still-water qualification test with a shared straight reference, both planners completed the empty-water case. With one prescribed crossing vessel, both exposed cases observed the approaching target before its nominal line passage. VO reached the goal region, while DWA triggered the shared predictive brake and stopped at the next supported planning boundary without an observed collision. The partial DWA record is retained, and the computation budget is assessed separately from the fixed logical delivery used in the pilot.

在共同直线参考的静水资格试验中，两法均完成空场任务。加入同一绝对时钟的横越船后，两例都在来船过线前观察到接近。VO 到达，DWA 触发共同预测制动，随后在下个支持域规划边界停止，未观察到碰撞。保留截短记录；实测计算预算与固定逻辑交付分别评估。

可作为 RQ2 的一个条件化预实验对照。空场 DWA 较早到达，交叉 DWA 不应把 30 s 截短当成任务耗时。两 VO 格同样 89.85 s 到达并不证明策略或航迹一致，交叉 VO 的到达也不能单独证明某次主动避碰带来的收益。两套具体实施、一个地图/来船相位/种子，未覆盖 RL、流风浪或 COLREG 合规。
